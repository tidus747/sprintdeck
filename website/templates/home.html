WHY IT EXISTS

The conversation is the work.
The screen should support it.

A planning board shows the work. The meeting also needs a goal, a review order, a timebox, decisions, and a record of what was agreed. When those live in different tabs, the facilitator has to keep moving the team's attention between them.

SprintDeck brings that meeting context into one visual workspace. Your board stays visible through OBS. Each person gets a structured review. Agreements stay visible, and the session ends with a written recap.

A small layer of meeting structure, independent of the software that tracks your tasks.

BEFORE, DURING, AFTER

Make the ceremony easier to follow.

01 / PREPARE

Start with the same context.

Put the sprint goal, dates, holidays, planned absences, and roadmap milestones in view before individual reviews begin.

02 / FACILITATE

Keep one clear thread.

Move through the team without switching presentation scenes. Checklists stay separate, shared objectives stay put, and timers keep accurate time.

03 / CLOSE

Leave with a usable record.

Confirm scope, record risks and decisions, assign next actions, and download the complete notes and agreements as a Markdown recap.

YOUR WORKFLOW, YOUR DEVICE

Keep your board.
Keep your data local.

SprintDeck runs on your computer and serves the controller and overlays on localhost. There is no cloud account, telemetry, or required connection to Jira or any other task platform.

Read the privacy model ↗
01

Presentation and control stay separate.

Share OBS output with your meeting. Keep editing controls and private session notes on the facilitator's screen.

02

Your existing board remains the source.

OBS captures the application separately. Capacity stays there. SprintDeck adds meeting context rather than another task-management model.

03

Readable exports, simple persistence.

Save a JSON backup for later, and a Markdown recap for the team. No external service is needed to record the agreements.

A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before you start.

Does it replace Jira or another planning board?

No. Keep your existing board and capture it with OBS. SprintDeck handles the meeting structure: goals, reviews, timers, notes, and commitments. It has no project management API integration.

Do I need an OBS plugin or WebSocket connection?

No. Use ordinary OBS Browser Sources and Window Capture or Display Capture. The setup guide covers source order, transparency, and uniform 16:9 scaling.

Can I use this website as the live controller?

No. This is a static documentation website. The live controller runs locally with Python, and session data stays on the presenting computer.

What should I check before a real meeting?

Test your capture alignment, scene transitions, audio setup, and audience readability in your meeting software. Browser checks are included; actual OBS composition still needs a rehearsal.
